## Not included

Three models used during development are not redistributable and are not in this repository: the WLKATA Haro380 arm (a commercial product), an Ender 3 V2 STEP model re-uploaded to Printables with no license, and a printed parallel gripper of unknown origin. `examples/step/Haro380.spec.yaml` is the hand-written spec for the Haro380; it needs your own copy of the STEP file.

## Dependencies

Python dependencies (build123d / OpenCascade, trimesh, MuJoCo, CoACD, PyBullet, SAPIEN, ManiSkill and others) are installed from PyPI under their own licenses and are not bundled. Isaac Sim is an optional separate install under NVIDIA's license terms.
